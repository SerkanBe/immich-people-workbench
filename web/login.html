People Workbench

Connect to your Immich library. Credentials stay in Workbench memory until logout, eight-hour expiry or restart.

Saved drafts and Pending stay here. Reconnecting does not Sync them.

Required permissions

Create a key in your Immich account settings with these ten permissions:

  • user.read
  • person.read
  • person.statistics
  • person.update
  • person.create
  • person.merge
  • asset.read
  • asset.view
  • face.read
  • face.update

Use the account whose library you want to review. Extra permissions produce a warning. Workbench never creates or revokes keys.

Sign in with an Immich admin account. Workbench forwards your password to Immich and does not save it.
